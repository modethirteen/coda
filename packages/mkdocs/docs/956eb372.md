## How do I migrate to react-router 6?

It depends, if your app is:

* Easy - then you might [just go directly to react-router 6](https://reactrouter.com/en/main/upgrading/v5) using the example app without needing to use the compat router that the [react-router v6 migration guide](https://developer.appf.io/docs/default/domain/front-end-platform/migrations/migrate-to-react-router-6/) from AppFolio has. 👉 Make sure to follow the [end of the AppFolio migration guide](https://developer.appf.io/docs/default/domain/front-end-platform/migrations/migrate-to-react-router-6/#migration-strategy-for-new-features) to be able to leverage the latest features.
* Medium - You want to follow the [migration guide](https://developer.appf.io/docs/default/domain/front-end-platform/migrations/migrate-to-react-router-6/).
* Hard - You want to follow the [migration guide](https://developer.appf.io/docs/default/domain/front-end-platform/migrations/migrate-to-react-router-6/) and you will probably want to do it over several PRs. So this would leverage the compat router in production.

I am determining the above based on how much react-router usage there is in your app!

### How long will migration take?

These are my estimations, but here’s the criteria

* Easy - within the day to 1 day
* Medium - 2 - 3 days
* Hard - 4+ days
